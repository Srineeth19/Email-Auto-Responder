CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

ORDER STATUS AND DELIVERY
The current status of an order must be checked using the order status tool.
Do not guess delivery dates or tracking numbers.

ORDER CANCELLATION
Cancellation eligibility depends on the order status and the company's approved policy.
Do not promise cancellation unless the order system confirms it.
If the policy does not specify eligibility, the support team must verify it.

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Return windows and refund timelines must follow the company's approved policy.
Do not invent refund timelines.
Do not claim a refund has been issued unless the payment or order system confirms it.
If the policy does not provide the answer, the support team must verify it.

CUSTOMER ACCOUNT INFORMATION
Customer information should be retrieved from an approved customer information tool.
Do not invent customer names or customer IDs.

CUSTOMER EMAIL RESPONSES
Responses should be professional, concise, polite, and helpful.
Use retrieved knowledge for general support questions.
Use verified tool results for current order information.
If the knowledge base does not contain the answer, recommend verification by the support team.
